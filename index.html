The Daily Oath
Read aloud every morning. Before you touch your phone.

I am the commander of this mission. My team follows my energy, not my words. Today I will lead by example.

I will not leak my energy into things that do not serve the mission or my marriage. My eyes, my thoughts, and my attention belong to my wife and my work. Nothing else gets access today.

I will sit in God's Word for one full hour today. I will read, meditate, and let the Scripture shape my mind before the world tries to. My spirit leads. My flesh follows.

I will train my body because it is the engine that powers everything else. I will eat with purpose. I will sleep with discipline. I will treat my health like the ₦700M asset it is.

I will make my wife feel chosen today. Not with grand gestures but with presence, attention, and a genuine desire to know how she is doing. She is my first priority. The mission is my second.

I will make at least 3 sales conversations happen. I will review my team's output. I will move money closer to the account. This is a revenue day. Every day is a revenue day.

I will go to bed tonight knowing that I gave today everything I had. And tomorrow I will do it again.
